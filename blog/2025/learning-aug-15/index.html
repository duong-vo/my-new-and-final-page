Catching up on the market
A thing I want to note that all of the stocks I picked are underperforming badly during this booming period if it’s not for the other bank stocks. The old stock that I see over and over still drive the market (like VIX, LPB, SSI, VND, VIC, which are just not that great of the business). My value stock should underperform further and further, but I’m still early in my career so let’s see how this playout, Greenblatt says that the magic formula that overperform the market still suffer from big bouts of underperformance. Let’s wait and see. I think a good way to align with market is also buy extremely durable and good business (like MBB, STB, and future big player like TPB and LPB) to align with market, but only during market downturn, and hold it through to the bull market

News
Apparently, some companies are buying Bitcoins to reverse investor sentiment and sell their shares to raise money. I remain a skeptic on Bitcoin as a durable long term asset/collectible, or whatever (if it has any intrinsic value compared to gold or silver). I mean, being able to transfer money without a bank is great but is there any true value to it?.
Vietnam is migrating to electric vehicle (by the government), and electric bike are all produced by Vingroup currently, so another nudge for Vingroup to make a bunch of money. VIC is enterprise level now so should not affect much of its value currently

Other
Howard Marks says that as a value investor, you will see a lot of missed opportunities and he said that passing on an opportunity is better than buying a loser. Maybe true, may be not so true because your goal is to make money, and market calls, follow the money is sometimes the way you do it, while being contrarian only pays off in the long run. Apparently in the US there’s also a period of elevated price in 2005 until 2007, before a market crash hit. That’s almost 2 years of continuous booming, same with Vietnam. He said that he sold in middle of 2005, and he does not regret it because it’s better than the people who hold way past 2007, but that’s not the right mentality as an investor.
