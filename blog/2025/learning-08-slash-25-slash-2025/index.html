Learning
There is analysis about what makes a good bank by John Huber (https://sabercapitalmgt.com/wells-fargo-
vs-small-community-banks/)
A good yardstick for earnings about bank is actually PTPP (Pre-tax, pre-provision earnings), as well as pre tax earnings. Like its name, earnings before tax and money set aside (provisions) for future credit loss. A good yard stick is to take an average rather than care about abnormally high provisions, which often create confusion among lesser knowledgable investor.
High ROE and Pretax return on tangible equity (ROTE), obviously, higher rate of return on capital means the bank can make more money with similar investment, in turn, higher stock price growth organically
A new thing I learn is the cost of deposits, meaning banks with lower interest rate often will have lower cost, in turn they can use that money into their high ROE and generate higher money, despite lower interest rate mean the bank is less attractive but money from deposits is not the key indicator of banks.
A final big thing is the economy of scale (an economy), meaning bigger the bank, the lower the cost and also wider network across the country.
So next time look at bank, look at the cost, the assets as well as the number of bank network, the ROE, ROTE, during the depressed market.
